THE STORY BEHIND CFP

Good Grains.
Better Snacking.

At Chaudhary Farm Products, we believe that the ingredients we grow up with can inspire the way we snack today. Millets are little-known treasures of our food heritage, versatile, nourishing grains with so much to offer. We’re here to celebrate their goodness and make discovering millet-based snacks an enjoyable part of everyday life.

Familiar Grains.
New Cravings.

“From savoury crunch to little sweet treats, there's more than one way to enjoy millets.”

Every snack has its own personality. Some are crisp and savoury, some are rich and bite-sized, and some bring a little sweetness to the day.

Our range explores the many ways millets can be enjoyed, from crunchy savoury favourites to comforting little treats. We believe discovering these grains should feel exciting, approachable and delicious, not like a compromise.

LITTLE TUMMIES. BIG POSSIBILITIES.

Kids Deserve More Than Just Snacks.
They Deserve Grain Goodness.

Growing up comes with big appetites, little cravings and a whole lot of energy. But finding snacks that are fun for little ones and made with thoughtful ingredients can be a challenge.

That's where India's homegrown grains come in. From calcium-rich ragi to fibre-containing jowar and nutrient-rich bajra, these traditional millets have so much to bring to the table.

At CFP, we're giving these familiar grains a new spin: turning them into crunchy, munchable and delicious snacks that make exploring the goodness of millets exciting for kids and reassuring for parents.

Because better snacking shouldn't feel like a compromise. It should feel like one more bite of something wonderful.
