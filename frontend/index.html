THE NUTRITIONAL POWER OF MILLETS

Ancient Indian grains with naturally occurring fibre, protein, minerals, and other nutrients. Discover what Ragi, Jowar, and Bajra bring to the table.

NATURALLY OCCURRING FIBRE

High Dietary Fibre

Millets contain dietary fibre, with amounts varying by grain and product. Fibre contributes to a balanced diet.

GRAIN-BASED NOURISHMENT

Plant-Based Protein

Ragi, Jowar, and Bajra contribute plant-based protein. Their nutritional composition varies by grain and preparation.

Ragi • Jowar • Bajra

Three traditional grains. Different nutritional profiles.

BALANCED DIGESTION

Glycemic Response

The effect of a grain on blood glucose depends on the grain, processing, portion size, and how it is prepared and eaten.

NATURALLY GENTLE

Naturally Gluten-Free Grains

Ragi, Jowar, and Bajra are naturally gluten-free grains. Finished products should be described as gluten-free only when their ingredients and manufacturing controls support that claim.
